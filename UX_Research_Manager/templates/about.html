About UX Research Manager

About the UX Research Manager

The UX Research Manager is a simple tool designed to help teams organize, understand, and act on user research in one place. It allows researchers, designers, and developers to store UX insights, create personas, and summarize research notes so nothing gets lost during the design and development process.

The main goal of this app is to make user research more accessible and usable for businesses and product teams. By keeping research insights and personas organized, teams can make more informed design decisions, support user-centered thinking, and ensure that research findings are clearly communicated during developer handoff.

Core Features

  • 📝 Create and manage research insights
  • 👥 Define and link personas
  • 🗺️ Map insights to user journey stages
  • 🤖 AI-powered research summarization
  • 💾 Persistent data storage

Technology Stack

Built with Python, Flask, and powered by Mistral AI

How to Use the App (Step-by-Step)

  1. Start at the Dashboard
    The dashboard gives you a quick overview of your UX research data and lets you navigate to insights or personas.
  2. Create UX Research Insights
    Use the "Create Insight" page to add research findings from interviews, surveys, or usability tests. Each insight acts like a tag or reference point that can be revisited later during design decisions.
  3. Create and Manage Personas
    Personas help represent key user types. You can create personas based on your research and update them as you learn more about your users.
  4. View and Edit Data
    All insights and personas can be viewed, edited, or deleted at any time. This makes the app flexible as research evolves.
  5. Use AI Summaries (If Enabled)
    When available, AI can help summarize longer research notes, making it easier to spot patterns and key takeaways.

Why This Matters for User Research & Testing

This app supports user research and usability testing by giving teams a centralized place to store findings. Instead of scattered notes or documents, research insights stay connected and easy to reference throughout the product lifecycle.

For businesses and companies, this means:

  • Better alignment between research and design decisions
  • Easier collaboration between designers, researchers, and developers
  • Clear documentation that supports product improvements based on real user feedback

Supporting Developer Handoff

By organizing UX insights and personas in a structured way, the app helps bridge the gap between design and development. Developers can easily understand user needs, assumptions, and research-backed decisions, leading to smoother handoffs and more user-centered implementations.
